Aaniyah's Favorite Movies

My top five movies of all time(not in order)

Home Alone 1 & 2

I don't celebrate Christmas, but this has to be one of my all time favorite movies. These movies bring me comfort that I just can't put into words. It's cozy, delightful, and just amazing. 10/10 would recommend.

13 Going on 30

Another classic that I love. Especially now that I am older. This movie makes me feel nostalgic and sometimes I wish I could go back in time to enjoy my childhood again and really appreciate the time in which I got to grow up. Life really was better back then.

White Chicks

I could never not put this movie on my list. This is THE classic comedy movie. It's timeless and I NEVER get tired of watching it. Every time I watch this movie, I still laugh at every joke. This movie just never gets old.

Train to Busan

I'm not a big horror movie enthusiast but this has to be one of my favorite horror movies. It's heartbreaking and suspenseful at the same time. I always get so mad and annoyed everytime I watch this movie because I wish there could have been an alternate ending that satisfied me. Still one of the best horror movies out there.

Spiderman: Brand New Day

Has to be my favorite spiderman movie. Tom Holland was CHEF's KISS. I cried like 5 times watching this movie. It was so sad but also very touching. And the cinematics for it were AWESOME!!

Barbie Movies

When I say "Barbie Movies", I mean like the old movies I watched when I was a kid. Like Barbie: Princess and the Pauper, or Barbie: Mermadia, or Barbie and the 12 Dancing Princesses. Not the new Barbie stuff. No! Nothing Barbie now can beat Barbie back then.
